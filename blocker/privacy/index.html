Sabaq Blocker privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Sabaq Blocker is a browser extension that works with Sabaq Study Desk (sabaq-study.pages.dev). While the Sabaq focus timer runs, it blocks the sites the student chose in Sabaq. This policy explains what it handles.

What it stores

  • The list of sites to block, the time the current focus round ends, and the language of the Sabaq page.
  • This is kept only in the browser's own extension storage on your device, and is cleared when the round ends.

What it reads

  • When a focus round starts, it checks the web addresses of your open tabs, so a tab already on a blocked site switches to the "Time to focus" page. Addresses are compared on your device and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • On the Sabaq website only, it receives the focus timer's state (on or off, the sites, the end time) from the page.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any personal information or browsing history.
  • It does not send any data to Sabaq, to the developer or to anyone else. It makes no network requests of its own.
  • It does not use analytics, ads or remote code.

Contact
