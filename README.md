# Sabaq Study Desk

A homework helper for students, with the page in English, العربية, Español, اردو, हिन्दी, বাংলা, Français and 中文.

**Open the app: https://albaraaroury190-tech.github.io/sabaq/**

## What works on this website

- Homework list with tickboxes, due dates and reminders
- A schedule of what is due each day: tap a day to see it and set goals for it
- A focus timer with your own focus and break lengths, counting minutes per assignment

Everything is saved in your own browser on your device.

The AI features (answers, study plans, flashcards, quizzes, read-aloud and voice typing) only work when the page is opened inside Claude.

## Files

`index.html` is the whole app in one file, with no build step.
